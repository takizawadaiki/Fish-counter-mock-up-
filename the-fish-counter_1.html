Our Story

Born from a passion for the sea

The Fish Counter was born from a deep love of sustainable seafood. We opened our doors with a simple mission: to serve fresh, delicious meals that protect our waters for future generations.

Every item on our menu is Ocean Wise certified — because great food and a healthy ocean go hand in hand. Together, we're making waves for a better tomorrow, one bite at a time.
